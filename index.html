02 · How It Works

Three steps.
Under a second.

Keyboard Saver maps every physical key on your keyboard to its Arabic and Kurdish equivalents, and the reverse. When you paste text that looks wrong, it detects the script mismatch and shows you what you actually meant to type.

1
Type or Paste
Drop in any text, even mid-sentence gibberish. No formatting needed.
→
2
Auto-Detection
The tool identifies which language the keyboard was set to when you typed.

3
All Three Outputs
English, Arabic, and Kurdish Sorani, all shown at once. Copy the one you need.
