How it works
English (QWERTY / AZERTY / QWERTZ / Dvorak)
Select your physical keyboard layout, type or paste, and get Arabic and Kurdish equivalents instantly.
q → ض (AR) · ق (KU)
h → ا (AR) · ه (KU)
? → ؟ (AR/KU)
Arabic (عربي)
Paste Arabic-layout keystrokes typed while English was active — recover both English and Kurdish automatically.
ض → q (EN)
ص → w (EN)
، → , (EN)
Kurdish Sorani (کوردی)
Exclusive Kurdish chars (ە، ێ، ۆ، ڕ، ڤ) identify Kurdish input precisely and convert to both other scripts.
ق → q (EN)
ە → e (EN)
ۆ → o (EN)

🔒 Privacy
where your pasted data goes

Nowhere. The whole website doesn't save any info. Conversion happens entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to any server, logged, stored, or analysed.

We don't track visits. We don't use analytics. We don't even want your cookies — there are none to give.

Paste your passwords, your secrets, your embarrassing autocorrect disasters. It stays between you and your screen.
